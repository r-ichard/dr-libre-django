# Product

DrLibre schedules appointments for several hospitals. Source of truth: `drlibre-specs.md`, section 16.

## Who uses it

- **Visitor and Patient:** often unwell, worried or in a hurry, on a phone. They come to find a provider and book without effort. A Patient signs in with no password.
- **Clinician (public word: provider):** between consultations. Comes to see the day, set availability and time off.
- **Admin:** manages hospitals, departments and rules. Comes to configure and fix.

## Voice

Plain, warm, precise. Sentence case. No marketing text, no exclamation marks. Controls name their action ("Sign in", "Book an appointment"). Errors name the problem and the way out. Use the domain words exactly; say "provider" where a user reads it.
