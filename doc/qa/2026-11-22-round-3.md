### #33 P1: Clinician form answers 500 for a round robin weight above 32767

#### Summary
The clinician create and edit forms in the admin console answer HTTP 500 when the round robin weight is above 32767. The field has no upper bound in the form, so the database rejects the value (`DataError`).

#### Steps to reproduce
1. `docker compose up --wait`, seed the demo, sign in as `admin@example.test`.
2. Open `/admin-console/clinicians/<id>/edit` for any clinician.
3. Post the form (valid token, `Origin` set) with `round_robin_weight=32768` (also `2147483647`, `99999999999` on `/admin-console/clinicians/new`).
4. Compare with `32767`, which saves.

#### Observed
HTTP 500, our error page with a reference. `scratch/qa/server.log` holds `"message": "unexpected error"` with exception type `DataError`. Nothing saved.

#### Expected
A validation message on the field, as rooms and appointment types give ("Use a whole number of at most 32767"). Section 4: validation at every boundary; expected failures are results (`invalid_input`), not crashes.

#### Evidence
Reproduced as second reader: edit post with `32768` gave 500, reference `26129761a0c1450390aa2fef6767e38c`, server log `DataError`; the stored weight stayed 1.

### #34 P2: Admin booking and reschedule errors show internal rule codes

#### Summary
When an admin books or reschedules an appointment at a time that breaks a booking rule, the error page prints the internal rule codes. An admin cannot act on "outside_availability".

#### Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in as `admin@example.test`, open any BOOKED appointment `<id>`.
2. `POST /admin-console/appointments/<id>/reschedule/review?starts_at=2030-03-05T01:00:00Z` with a valid token and `Origin` (or book for a patient at a time outside availability or in the past).

#### Observed
422 page "This change was not made. Error. outside_availability". A past time reads "start_in_the_past" (joined with other codes by spaces).

#### Expected
A sentence, as conflicts get ("The clinician is no longer free then."). Section 4: a rule violation lists the violated rules; section 10 asks for a reason and a way forward. Cause: `_message_and_status` in `scheduling/views/pages/admin_refusal.py` joins the codes as text.

#### Evidence
Reproduced as second reader, reference `cd6483511fca4346b8d6c9bd862f0dea`; the appointment stayed BOOKED.

#### Summary
A status action that is not allowed from the current status (complete a BOOKED appointment, check in twice, cancel a COMPLETED one) says "The status changed since this page was opened". Nothing changed; the action was never allowed, and the current status is not named.

#### Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in as `admin@example.test`; take a BOOKED appointment `<id>`.
2. `POST /admin-console/appointments/<id>/complete` with a valid token and `Origin`.

Reproduced as second reader, reference `8d42f597a6664d67a77bdc5207477759`.

### #36 P2: Changing a hospital time zone moves existing appointments with no warning or notice

#### Summary
Saving a hospital with a new time zone silently moves the local time of every existing appointment, with no warning to the admin, no notice to patients, and an email that said something else.

#### Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in as `admin@example.test`. Open an upcoming appointment of a Berlin clinic: it reads "Monday 23 November 2026, 13:00 to 13:15 (Europe/Berlin)".
2. Edit the hospital, set time zone `Pacific/Auckland`, save (303, no warning).
3. Reopen the appointment (and the patient's `/patient/appointments`).

#### Observed
"Tuesday 24 November 2026, 01:00 to 01:15 (Pacific/Auckland)". The instant is unchanged; availability is wall-clock, so the appointment now sits outside any rule. No Notification is created; the confirmation email said 13:00.

#### Expected
Section 4 shows times in the hospital's zone; the specification does not say what happens to existing appointments. Raised as a gap: at least warn the admin on the form with the count of future appointments affected, and decide (owner) whether patients are told.

#### Evidence
Reproduced as second reader on appointment `01a202de-a400-790a-b25e-baa79db45323`; zone restored afterwards.

9d2aee0
